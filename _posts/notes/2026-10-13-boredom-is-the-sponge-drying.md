title: Boredom is the sponge drying
---

A reminder to myself: You need to let the sponge of your brain dry out if you expect to absorb anything new. Having a steady drip drip drip of more content, queues of article tabs open, saved podcasts to sprinkle in at any 8 minute opening.. it all leads to saturation. Some ideas might be getting in there, but what do you want to stick? Most will just roll off the top and drip away. When you do come across something you really want to absorb, you won't be able to, you'll be full and dripping.

Getting too dry is the uncomfortable bored feeling, but that is probably better than being always full.

During college, you walk the line near saturation, but you still get breaks during holidays, can slack off, rotate the sponge to dry parts by taking new classes.

Spaced repetition is the other trick to ensure ideas stick to your core: they may have rolled off the first two or five times you tried absorbing them, but if you just keep reapplying at a certain interval, the ideas will stick.

See also [Do less](https://usefulfictions.substack.com/p/do-less).
